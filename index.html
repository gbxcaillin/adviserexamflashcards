How it works

Three ways to interact with each card.

↻
Tap to flip
See the answer or definition on the back.
→
Swipe right = Known
Card moves on. You won't see it in still-learning.
←
Swipe left = Still learning
Card comes back to the end of the deck and is saved for review later.

How to use

1
Choose your deck size
Pick 10, 20, 50 or 100 cards at the top of the home screen. Start with 20 for a focused study sprint, or go to 100 for a full mock session.
2
Pick a mode
Random — draws from all 100 cards across every topic.
Statement cards — section number on the front, rule on the back. Tests recall of what each section does.
Question cards — asks which section covers a topic. Tests recall in reverse.
By Category — drill one topic at a time (APPs, FASEA Standards, Licensing, etc).
3
Work through the cards
Tap the card to flip it and see the answer. Swipe right if you know it — card is marked Known and moves on. Swipe left if you're still learning — card cycles back to the end of the deck this session and is saved for later review.
4
Use Previous & Next to navigate
The Previous and Next buttons let you move through cards without committing a Known or Learning verdict — useful for a first pass to familiarise yourself with the content.
5
Review your session summary
At the end of each session you'll see your score, a breakdown of which topics need work, and a shortcut to immediately drill your weak areas.
6
Practice Still-Learning
Cards you swiped left are saved across sessions. Use the Practice Still-Learning button on the home screen to run a focused deck of only the cards you haven't mastered yet.
7
Print physical cards
Hit Print Cards (PDF) to open a print-ready layout — 4 cards per A4 page, front and back side by side, with cut lines. Save as PDF from your browser's print dialog.
